# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is Wellface?**
Wellface is a medical aesthetics clinic group in Surrey and South West London, specialising in injectable treatments (Botox, fillers), skin rejuvenation, laser treatments, and advanced facials. All injectable treatments are performed by qualified doctors.

**Where are your clinics?**
We have four locations: Cobham, Esher, Wimbledon, and East Horsley. See [locations.md](locations.md) for full addresses, hours, and contact details.

**Do I need a consultation first?**
Yes — if you're a new patient, a consultation is required before most treatments. This ensures the treatment is right for you and allows our practitioners to create a personalised plan. Some consultations are free; others require a deposit.

**What age do I need to be?**
You must be 18 or over for injectable treatments. Teenage Hydrafacials are available for younger clients.

## Treatments

**Does Botox hurt?**
Most patients describe a slight pinch. The needles used are very fine and the procedure is quick (around 20 minutes). Numbing cream can be applied if needed.

**How long does Botox last?**
Typically 3–4 months. Results become visible within 7–14 days.

**How long do fillers last?**
Depending on the area and product, fillers typically last 6–18 months. Lip fillers usually last 6–12 months.

**What is Profhilo?**
Profhilo is a bio-remodelling treatment that uses hyaluronic acid to stimulate collagen and elastin. It improves skin hydration, firmness, and overall quality. Results last around 6 months.

**What is a Hydrafacial?**
A multi-step facial that cleanses, extracts, and hydrates the skin using patented technology. Suitable for all skin types with no downtime.

**Is there downtime after treatments?**
- **Botox**: No downtime. Avoid lying down for 4 hours.
- **Fillers**: Mild swelling and bruising for 1–3 days.
- **Hydrafacial**: No downtime — immediate glow.
- **Morpheus8 / CO2 Laser**: 3–7 days of redness and peeling.
- **Chemical peels**: 2–5 days of peeling depending on depth.

**How often should I get a Hydrafacial?**
Every 4–6 weeks for optimal results.

**How often should I get Botox?**
Every 3–4 months to maintain results.
