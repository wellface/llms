# Gift Cards

Wellface gift cards make the perfect present for someone who deserves a treat.

## How it works

- Available in **any amount** (GBP)
- Can be used for **any treatment or product** at any Wellface clinic
- Valid for **24 months** from purchase
- Can be partially redeemed (the balance carries forward)

## Delivery options

- **Email** — sent instantly to the recipient
- **Scheduled email** — delivered on a chosen date (great for birthdays)
- **Postal** — a physical card posted to an address

## Redemption

Present your gift card code (format: WF-XXXX-XXXX) when you arrive for your appointment. The amount will be deducted from your treatment cost. Any remaining balance stays on the card for future use.
